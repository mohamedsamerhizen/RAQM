# Capital expenditure and acquisitions

An acquisition or improvement request records business reason and procurement evidence against the asset and its category. The Owner approves the capitalization threshold, component allocation and source treatment before posting. Eligible cost includes documented directly attributable expenditure and applicable nonrecoverable amounts. Recoverable input tax stays in its own account. Ordinary servicing belongs in expense. [IAS 16](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-16-property-plant-and-equipment/)

| Funding method | Required evidence | GL effect when posting the asset operation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direct | Approved evidence and an active registered bank/cash GL or eligible ordinary liability offset | Debit fixed asset cost, credit the selected offset. |
| Existing cost | Whole unused net-cost debit lines on the category cost account from posted, uncredited supplier bills | Attach the lines to the register. |
| Clear bill | Whole unused net-cost debit lines on the category clearing account from posted, uncredited supplier bills | Debit asset cost and credit clearing for the same amount. |

The amounts claimed must exactly equal the approved capital amount. A linked supplier, if specified, must match the bill. A bill may include discounts and recoverable input tax. Claim the posted net asset cost line after discounts. The existing payable and tax journal remains the financial evidence for that bill. No supplier obligation or receipt is recreated by a claim. Use one funding mode per operation. Multiple eligible lines in that mode can represent directly attributable costs. A consumed bill is protected from later ordinary supplier credits until an explicit coordinated correction workflow is available.

New acquisitions allocate the entire amount across the components. Improvements add cost to a selected component at a fiscal period start and can revise its future useful life and residual. A replacement that requires derecognition of an existing component is rejected. For repairs, either claim an eligible posted expense line or post the expense against registered cash using an explicitly selected expense account. Repairs do not change cost. If an unclaimed supplier bill uses asset control in error, use the existing supplier credit and a corrected bill before capitalization.

## Opening conversion

Register the assets and components with historical cost, accumulated depreciation, impairment, remaining useful life and acquisition evidence. Prepare an opening batch with its original posted Phase 01 opening journal. An evidenced historical availability date may be supplied. An Owner approves the entire batch only when the category totals reconcile to the posted GL at the chosen cutover after accounting for existing register balances. A partial batch with an unexplained difference fails. Posting adds register movements and the source link without adding a second opening journal. The cutover date must be in an open accounting period and must follow any posted depreciation cutoff it would otherwise change.
